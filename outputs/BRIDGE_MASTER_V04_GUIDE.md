Style: simple student pixel art matching source palette: orange/peach sky, slate navy metal, violet/blue city, sparse cyan accents. Broad flat fills with minimal shadow shades, chunky pixels, very few rectangular windows, simple water bands/reflections. Avoid complex texture/realism. Preserve calm pre-tragedy dawn mood.
